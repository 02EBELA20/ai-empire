# Milestone 4 — Prioritized Production Order

1. **Shared visual foundation:** floor tiles, palette, atlas, material shader presets, fixed-camera mobile framing. Quickest jump from the flat slab.
2. **gpu_rack S1–S4:** most frequently upgraded visual hero; prove stage switching and fan instancing.
3. **ai_core S1–S4:** central landmark, readable long-term aspiration and build reveal.
4. **power_gen and cooling S1–S4:** establish visibly connected cause-and-effect and distinct flow colors.
5. **network_hub and basic_pc S1–S4:** complete the six-device family and starter tutorial.
6. **Connection polish:** cable trench routing, three profiles, cheap directional flow, throttled warning and just-built sequence.
7. **Mobile performance gate:** test full representative scene under 100 draw calls and 150k triangles, fixed camera and 30 FPS on actual mid-range Android. Reduce props and FX first if needed.
8. **UI pass:** device bottom sheet, upgrade anticipation, state warnings, coherent shop cards and accessible typography.

**Review gates:** silhouette sheet approval → one integrated vertical slice (GPU + power + cooling) → full six-device coverage → performance signoff. No mass modeling until the vertical slice passes.
